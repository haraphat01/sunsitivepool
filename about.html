Nearly two decades keeping Austin pools running

Our work

Precision workmanship. Honest recommendations.

Our comprehensive services include complete heater replacement to restore consistent, efficient heating, energy-efficient pump replacement to improve circulation and reduce utility costs, and high-quality filter replacement to maintain crystal-clear water and proper system flow. When your equipment shows signs of wear or failure, we quickly identify the root cause and provide solutions that prevent recurring problems.

We also specialize in salt system repair and installation to ensure balanced, effective sanitation and softer water quality. Our advanced automation system setup and troubleshooting services allow you to conveniently control pumps, heaters, lighting, and other features with ease. In addition, we perform expert PVC repair and valve repair to correct leaks, restore proper water flow, and prevent costly structural or equipment damage.

Our commitment

Factory-trained. Warranty-compliant.

As a Jandy Pro Authorized Technician, Sunsitive Pools is factory-trained to install, service, and maintain Jandy equipment according to strict manufacturer standards. This ensures warranty-compliant workmanship, accurate system configuration, and dependable long-term performance. Since 2005, our commitment has remained the same: deliver exceptional service, quality craftsmanship, and peace of mind so your pool stays clean, efficient, and ready to enjoy whenever you are.
