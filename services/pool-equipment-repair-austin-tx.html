Pool pumps are often the first component to fail because they operate daily. If your pump is humming but not running, making grinding noises, or losing prime, immediate attention is required. Ignoring these symptoms can result in motor burnout. Our team evaluates whether repair or replacement is the smarter financial decision based on equipment age and condition.

Heater problems are also common in Austin due to mineral buildup and heavy seasonal use. If your heater cycles on and off, fails to ignite, or struggles to maintain temperature, a professional inspection is necessary. Timely repair prevents more extensive internal damage.

Salt systems require proper maintenance to continue generating chlorine efficiently. If chlorine levels drop despite balanced chemicals, the salt cell or control system may be failing. Correct diagnosis ensures the right component is repaired or replaced without unnecessary expense.

Automation systems add convenience, but when they malfunction, they can disrupt your entire setup. Programming errors, communication failures, and sensor issues can prevent pumps and heaters from operating correctly. Professional automation repair restores full control and prevents equipment strain.
